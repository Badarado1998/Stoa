# Terms and privacy

The terms of use for the Stoa interface and how it handles your data.

## Terms of use

**Your responsibility.** You decide every transaction and sign it in your own wallet. Check the token addresses, amounts, minimum received and price impact before you sign. Tokens imported by address are not reviewed by Stoa and may be worthless or malicious.

See [Risks and safeguards](risks.md).

**Fees.** Swaps pay a 2 bps protocol fee, plus the fee of any outside venue used and network gas. See [Fees](fees.md).

## Privacy

- **No account and no tracking.** Stoa has no sign-up, sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.
- **Stored in your browser only.** If you open a referral link, the referrer's address is kept in your browser's local storage so later swaps credit them. Clearing site data removes it.
- **Blockchain data is public.** Your wallet address and every transaction you sign are visible on Elysium to anyone, permanently. Stoa reads them from the chain to show your balances and swap history.
- **Services that see your requests.** The site is hosted on Vercel, fonts load from Google Fonts, and the app talks to Elysium RPC providers (such as Kinetiq, Alchemy and dRPC). Like any website, these providers see your IP address and the requests your browser makes. If you connect through WalletConnect, its relay sees the connection.
