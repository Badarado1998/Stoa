# Portfolio

Your Stoa balances and earnings in one view.

[Portfolio](https://stoadex.com/portfolio) shows the connected wallet's balances on the network you picked, the referral earnings you can claim, your swaps, and your profit and loss.

## Profit and loss

For every token you bought through Stoa on the network you picked, Portfolio shows what it cost, what you sold for, what you still hold and what the router would pay for it now, and the profit or loss in the gas token (ETH or HYPE), with the dollar value at today's price.

- Cost is your average price across your buys. Selling counts against that average.
- Only swaps through Stoa that paid your wallet count. Tokens bought elsewhere, or moved in from another wallet, are not included, and swaps between two tokens with no ETH, HYPE or stablecoin side are left out.
- Swaps against the stablecoin (USDG or USDC) count at today's ETH or HYPE price.

## Weekly streak

Portfolio shows how many weeks in a row the connected wallet has swapped through Stoa (weeks run Monday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 UTC). The current week counts as soon as you swap in it; before that, the streak stays alive until the week ends. It is a badge you can share, nothing more: it earns no [points](points.md).

## Notifications

On a phone with Stoa added to the home screen, or in a desktop browser, **Notify me on this device** (on the watchlist and the Spotlight page) sends a notification when a Spotlight week opens or its votes are about to close, when Spotlight leaves ETH for the connected wallet to claim, and when one of your price alerts is reached, even with Stoa closed. It sends Stoa this device's notification address, the connected wallet's address and your price alerts, nothing else; turning it off deletes them. On iPhone it needs Stoa added to the home screen first.
