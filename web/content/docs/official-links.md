# Official links and scams

Stoa's team **never sends the first direct message**, on X, Telegram, Discord or anywhere else. If someone writes to you first and says they are Stoa, Stoa support, a Stoa admin or a Stoa partner, it is not Stoa.

## The only official places

- Website: **stoadex.com**, and nothing else. Check the address bar before you connect a wallet.
- X: [@StoaDEX](https://x.com/StoaDEX)
- Telegram: [t.me/StoaDex](https://t.me/StoaDex) (announcements) and [t.me/StoaDexChat](https://t.me/StoaDexChat) (community chat)
- GitHub: [github.com/StoaDex/Stoa](https://github.com/StoaDex/Stoa)
- $STOA on Robinhood Chain: `0x13E6993417616De724F2b337D65063f229e96718`. Every other Stoa contract is listed on [Contracts](contracts.md).

## What Stoa never asks for

- Your seed phrase, private key, or a wallet "sync", "validation" or "unlock". No real service needs these.
- A payment, deposit or signature to fix a problem, unlock a reward, review a security report or get a token listed.
- Money sent to a wallet for a Spotlight slot. Spotlight bids go only into the [Spotlight contract](spotlight.md), from the Spotlight page on stoadex.com, and a bid that does not win is refunded by the contract.

## Common tricks

- **Fake support.** You ask a question in a group, and a "support agent" DMs you with a link to "fix" your wallet. Real admins answer in the group.
- **Fake security researchers.** A DM says it found a bug in Stoa and wants a reward, a call, or for you to open a file. Real reports go privately through GitHub, as described on [Security reports](bug-bounty.md), and DMs about them are not answered.
- **Look-alike sites and airdrops.** A site with a name close to Stoa's offers a claim, a bonus or a "migration". Stoa has no claim page: [Points](points.md) are shown on stoadex.com/points and need no signature.
- **Prize "claims".** Stoa's build bounties pay only in USDG on Robinhood Chain, to a wallet posted on the entry's GitHub issue by the account that opened it. A look-alike account posting its own address is never paid, and nobody needs to pay or sign anything to receive a prize.
- **Renamed accounts.** An account changes its name and picture to look like Stoa or someone from the community. On X, a "username changed recently" warning in a DM is a strong sign.

## If you are not sure

Ask in the [community chat](https://t.me/StoaDexChat), in public. If you signed something you should not have, move what is left in that wallet to a new wallet right away, and [revoke approvals](https://revoke.cash) from the old one.
